# Urban and Space recordings

Supplied by Mark from the Urban and Space folders in Downloads. Ambience files are loops; Sounds files are effects. Originals are unchanged.

Converted from OGG to LAME VBR quality 2 MP3 at 48 kHz, retaining mono/stereo channels. Loop files use equal-power wraparound crossfades; effects retain their full duration with at most 24 ms of trailing codec-frame padding. Constant gain sets the pre-encoding peak to -3 dBFS without dynamic compression.
